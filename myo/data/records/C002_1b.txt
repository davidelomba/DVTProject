A 31-year-old woman without any prior health conditions or cardiovascular
 risk factors was admitted in May 2021, about two weeks after her first mRNA
 COVID-19 vaccination, with episodes of chills, sharp retrosternal and dorsal
 pain, and shortness of breath during moderate exertion. The next day she
 experienced mild flu-like malaise, which resolved spontaneously. 
On examination, her blood pressure was within normal limits, and her
 heart rate was slightly elevated; no peripheral edema or Muffled heart
sounds was detected.  Laboratory investigations revealed raised cardiac
 biomarkers.  The ECG demonstrated ST-segment changes with lateral
 predominance. Echocardiography indicated a borderline reduction in left ventricular systolic performance (EF ≈ 50%) 
and regional wall motion disturbance involving the inferolateral wall.
 Longitudinal strain analysis showed global impairment (–11%). 
Cardiac MRI revealed abnormal tissue parameters (T1 ≈ 1180 ms, T2 ≈ 80 ms, ECV ≈ 35%)
 and a subepicardial enhancement pattern in the basal inferolateral myocardium on LGE imaging
, compatible with inflammatory injury. PCR screening for viral pathogens was negative,
 and coronary angiography excluded obstructive disease. Given the mildly depressed 
ventricular function, she was started on beta-blocker and angiotensin-receptor antagonist 
therapy. At discharge, she reported only positional chest discomfort without ischemic features.
 Follow-up echocardiography demonstrated normalized LV function and resolution of regional 
abnormalities. Revaccination with mRNA vaccine was not advised.
