Use this tool to search the patient's clinical record for any information relevant to a myocarditis or pericarditis diagnosis: cardiac symptoms (acute chest pain or pressure, palpitation, dyspnea at rest, with exercise or lying down, diaphoresis, sudden death), physical exam features (pericardial friction rub, pulsus paradoxus, distant heart sounds), inflammatory markers (ESR, CRP, high sensitivity CRP, D-dimer), electrocardiogram findings (atrial or ventricular arrhythmias, tachycardia, atrial fibrillation, AV block, bundle branch block, conduction delays, ST segment or T wave abnormalities, q waves, low voltage, premature atrial or ventricular contractions, ambulatory ECG monitoring), echocardiogram findings (ejection fraction, ventricular function, segmental wall motion, global systolic or diastolic function, ventricular dilation, wall thickness, pericardial fluid collection or inflammation), whether each test or examination was done at all, and any statement that it was normal.
