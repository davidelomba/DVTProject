Evaluate each option independently, on what the evidence states for this patient, in whatever language the record is written. Do not infer, assume or add any finding the evidence does not state.
Thresholds: ESR is elevated at or above 15 mm/hr for men under 50, 20 for men over 50 and for women under 50, 30 for women over 50, and above 2 for newborns, using the patient's age and sex; CRP is elevated above 10 mg/L; hsCRP above 3 mg/L; D-dimer above the laboratory's upper limit. If several values are reported, use the highest. Convert units before comparing: 1 g/L = 1000 mg/L; for D-dimer 1 µg/mL = 1 mg/L = 1000 ng/mL. Do not infer elevation or absence from symptoms alone. 'None of the above' requires that at least one of these four markers was measured and every measured one was within range; if none of them is mentioned, choose 'None tested, or tested but no results or unknown if tested'. Other tests are not among the markers of this section: troponin, CK-MB, white cell count, procalcitonin, and a PCR test for viruses (polymerase chain reaction), which is not CRP. Select '*confirmed positive inflammatory marker conforming myocarditis without details.' only if the record states that, or an equivalent, explicitly.
