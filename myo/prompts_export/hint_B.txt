Evaluate each option independently, on what the evidence states for this patient, in whatever language the record is written. Do not infer, assume or add any finding the evidence does not state.
Select a feature only if the physical examination in the evidence reports it: if a pericardial friction rub is mentioned, select it, and do not infer the other features from it. Distant heart sounds include muffled heart sounds. If no examination findings are reported at all, choose 'unknown if any of 1-3 present or absent'. Choose 'none of the above' only when the evidence states that these features were looked for and not found, for example 'no pericardial friction rub'. If the record states that the examination confirmed pericarditis without listing the findings, select only '*Confirmed physical exam conforming Pericarditis, without details'.
