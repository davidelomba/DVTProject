Evaluate each option independently, on what the evidence states for this patient, in whatever language the record is written. Do not infer, assume or add any finding the evidence does not state.
Consider only direct mentions of ECG features or results. If the ECG was not done or its result is not reported, choose only the first option. If a specific pattern is described (for example PACs, ST elevation, PR-segment depression), select only the option that names that pattern. Sinus tachycardia, SVT, atrial fibrillation, PVCs, VT and VF belong to 'Paroxysmal or sustained atrial or ventricular arrhythmias', as its examples say. Choose 'No abnormalities seen on ECG' only when the report states the ECG was normal or showed no abnormalities. Choose '*Nonspecific abnormalities other than those listed above' only for abnormalities the record explicitly calls nonspecific, such as 'nonspecific ST changes' or 'borderline low voltage'.
