Evaluate each option independently, on what the evidence states for this patient, in whatever language the record is written. Do not infer, assume or add any finding the evidence does not state.
Do not assume that similar terms or related conditions are equivalent unless the record says so: an arrhythmia seen on an ECG is not palpitation, and abdominal, shoulder or back pain are separate symptoms on this form (section A2), not chest pain. A symptom the evidence does not mention is unknown, not absent. Choose 'none of the above' only when the record explicitly states that the patient did not have these symptoms; when it says nothing about them, choose 'unknown if any of 1-5 present or absent'.
